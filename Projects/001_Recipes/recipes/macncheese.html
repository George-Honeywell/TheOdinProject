Mac 'n' Cheese with Bacon

Home

Image

Description

A rich, creamy macoroni cheese with bacon.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb of Grated Cheese (of your choice)
  • 1 lb of dry Macoroni
  • 4 strips of bacon, cut into small squares
  • 2 oz of flour
  • 2 oz of butter
  • 400ml of semi-skimmed milk

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to around 180 Degrees (C)
  2. Cook the pasta in hot, salted water. Feel free to rinse the dry pasta before boiling to remove any startch
  3. Put the bacon in a frying pan and cook until no more water is coming out
  4. When the pasta is cooked and soft, tip into a colender and set aside
  5. Put the 2 oz of butter into a sauce pan and stir until completely melted
  6. Once melted, add in the flour and milk. Stir until it starts to thicken
  7. Whilst waiting for the sauce to thicken, pour the cooked pasta into a large oven dish
  8. Add around 3/4 of the grated cheese and stir until the cheese has melted
  9. When the cheese has melted and the sauce looks smooth (no lumps), put in the bacon pieces and stir in (the heat can be turned off now)
  10. Pour the cheese sauce over the pasta in the oven dish and mix well
  11. With the remaining grated cheese, sprinkle it over the top
  12. Put in the oven for 15-20 minutes (or until golden brown on top)
  13. Remove from the oven and serve!

Personally, I like to cook garlic ciabatta bread with this! I also like sprinkling over Italian Seasoning and garlic sauce.
