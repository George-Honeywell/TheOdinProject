Description

Easy to make tortilla pizzas. A much healthier option than takeaway! ;)

Ingredients

  • Tortillas (I prefer mini/small tortillas)
  • 250g of Diced Chicken
  • 30g of grated cheese
  • BBQ or Tomato sauce
  • Chopped/Lazy Garlic
  • Any toppings of your choice (sweetcorn, chopped peppers, tomatoes, etc.)

Steps

  1. Put 2 tortillas on a baking tray and put in the oven at 150 degrees
  2. Place the diced chicken in a frying pan and put on the hob over a medium heat
  3. Whilst the chicken is cooking, be sure to check the tortillas in the oven and flip over every now and then
  4. Add garlic and other spices you want to the chicken and stir and leave to simmer
  5. Once the tortillas start to turn golden, take them out the oven. Personally, I wait until the tortillas are fairly hard to ensure they don't become soggy later on
  6. Let the tortillas cool for a few minutes, then add your base (BBQ or tomato). Spreading evenly
  7. Once the chicken is cooked, add to the top of the tortilla
  8. Add any toppings of your choice, including cheese!
  9. Put in the oven for around 5 minutes (or until the cheese has melted)
  10. Take out the oven and serve!
