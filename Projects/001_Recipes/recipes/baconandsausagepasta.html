Description

A nice comfort food, which is easy to make!

Ingredients

  • 4 Sausages of your choosing
  • 150g of dry pasta of your choice
  • 100g of grated cheddar cheese
  • 4 strips of bacon, cut into small pieces

Steps

  1. Start by slowly cooking the sausages in a frying pan (or oven)
    • If you choose to cook the sausages in a frying pan, make sure to turn them every few minutes!
  2. Whilst the sausages are cooking, start to boil the pasta in a sauce pan
  3. Keep the pasta on a low to medium heat, so it finishes cooking around the same time as the sausages
  4. Once the sausages are cooked from the outside, chop each one into 4 pieces
  5. When all sausages are cut, put them back in the frying pan and add the bacon
  6. When the pasta is cooked, drain off and set aside until the sausages and bacon are cooked
  7. When the sausages and bacon are cooked, mix it in with the pasta
  8. Put the contents into a bowl, add the grated cheese on top and serve!

To add a little bit of extra flavour, sprinkle over some italian seasoning and garlic sauce!
